# StowKit 1.4.0

Share documents and web pages to StowKit from any app, work through your Inbox one document at a
time or all at once, and collections that actually get assigned.

## Before you install: what is not proven

- **Tested on one Mac only**, the owner's (Apple Silicon, macOS 27). Household sharing is still
  experimental and untested with two iCloud accounts.
- **Share → StowKit must be switched on once** in System Settings → General → Login Items &
  Extensions → Sharing. Web pages are loaded without your Safari sign-ins, so a page behind a login
  saves as its login page, and images a page loads only as you scroll may come out blank.
- Long documents (more than roughly the first two pages of text) are still left in Inbox for you to
  confirm, by design; the review card and Organize Inbox make that quick.
- Organize Inbox's window was tested through its underlying logic; the owner's Inbox was empty when
  the release build was made.

## What's new

- **Share → StowKit.** From Finder, Preview, Mail, Safari, or any app's Share menu. PDFs and images are
  copied into your Inbox. A web page is saved as a paginated, searchable PDF named after the page. If
  StowKit isn't open, shared items wait and are imported the next time it is.
- **Collections get assigned.** Apple Intelligence was choosing sensible collections, but StowKit
  discarded them because the quote backing each choice had to match the document character for
  character, and the model reflows quotes. Quotes are now compared word by word; a quote containing
  a word the document doesn't have is still rejected.
- **Inbox review card.** In Inbox, each document shows its suggestion with **Use Suggestions & Next**
  (⌘↩), one-click buttons to file it in any collection, and **Mark Reviewed & Next** (⌥⌘↩). ⌘[ and ⌘]
  move between documents.
- **Organize Inbox** (⇧⌘I). Get suggestions for every Inbox document at once, change any collection,
  and apply them all together. Nothing changes until you press Apply.

## Good to know

- 1.3.0 updates itself to this version (StowKit → Check for Updates…). Earlier versions: install by
  hand.
- The sync format is unchanged.

## Installation

Open `StowKit-1.4.0.dmg` and drag StowKit to Applications, replacing the old version. Requires
macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple; use the SHA-256 sidecar to
verify the download.
