# StowKit 1.6.1

Documents whose text macOS refuses to read are no longer left blank: reading falls back to a second
recognizer, and then to Apple Intelligence on your Mac.

## Before you install: what is not proven

- **Apple Intelligence can invent text.** It is asked only when recognition has already failed, and
  what it reads is marked "Text read by Apple Intelligence", left for you to review, and never filed
  automatically — because on one of the owner's ID cards it produced a family name that is not on the
  card. Treat that text as a way to find a document, not as a record of what the document says.
- **Tested on one Mac only**, the owner's (Apple Silicon, macOS 27), on an archive of 24 documents,
  of which four needed the model. Its accuracy has not been measured against the documents themselves
  beyond the wrong name noted above.
- Everything 1.5.0 listed as unproven still is: Export, the paperless-ngx importer, Add Reminder,
  multi-select editing, saved views, and renaming a tag have not been used on a real archive. Household
  sharing remains untested across two iCloud accounts.
- A Mac without Apple Intelligence simply keeps the recognized text, as before.

## What's new

- **Text is read even when macOS text recognition fails.** On this Mac, recognition at its accurate
  level works once per app session and then fails for the rest of it — an Apple fault, reproduced
  outside StowKit. Reading now falls back to the fast level, which keeps working, so a document is
  never left with "Couldn't read text" because of it.
- **Apple Intelligence as a last resort.** A page that recognition turns into noise, or into a handful
  of words from a full scan, is transcribed from its image by Apple Intelligence on your Mac
  (1–3 seconds). Nothing is sent anywhere. The document says so and stays in your Inbox for review.
- **Confident filing needs text worth trusting.** A scan that yielded almost nothing is no longer filed
  automatically — that is what filed a birth certificate under Insurance as "life insurance coverage".
- **File → Read Text Again (⌥⌘R)** re-reads the selected documents from scratch.

## Good to know

- Documents that already failed keep their error until you re-read them: select them and press ⌥⌘R,
  or use Retry on the document.
- Re-reading fixes a document's details but does not remove a collection it was filed into earlier;
  remove those yourself with Change next to Collections.
- The archive and sync formats are unchanged from 1.5.0. Which pages a model read is kept on this Mac
  only and is not synced.

## Installation

Open `StowKit-1.6.1.dmg` and drag StowKit to Applications, replacing the old version. Requires
macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple; use the SHA-256 sidecar to
verify the download.
