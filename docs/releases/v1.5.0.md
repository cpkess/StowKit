# StowKit 1.5.0

Everything paperless-ngx users look for: document types, dates, amounts and due dates, filters and saved
views, people & things with related documents, reminders, multi-select editing, export, and an importer
for paperless-ngx exports.

## Before you install: what is not proven

- **Every Mac on your archive must update.** 1.5 adds synced document fields (type, amount, due date,
  expiry). A Mac still on 1.4 or earlier stops syncing when it sees them, until it updates. 1.5 opens
  your library in a new format that 1.4 cannot open, so there is no going back to 1.4 on this Mac.
- **Tested on one Mac only**, the owner's (Apple Silicon, macOS 27), with a five-document archive.
  Household sharing is still experimental and untested with two iCloud accounts.
- **Not yet tried on a real archive:** Export (tested only in the automated suite), Import from
  paperless-ngx (built from paperless-ngx's documented export format; no real paperless export has been
  imported), Add Reminder (no reminder has been created on a real Mac), multi-select editing, saved
  views, and renaming a tag.
- Dates, amounts, and names are read only from text StowKit found in the document; a figure or date
  that isn't in the text is never suggested. They are suggestions, not checked facts.

## What's new

- **Type, amount, due, and expires** on every document, suggested from its text (with Apple
  Intelligence or the built-in rules) and editable. **Upcoming** in the sidebar lists what is overdue,
  due, or expiring within 90 days.
- **Filters and saved views.** Filter by tag, person or thing, sender, type, period, or what's coming
  up; save any combination as a view in the sidebar. Tags and People & Things have their own sidebar
  sections, and a tag can be renamed across the archive.
- **People & things.** Names found in a document (people, companies, vehicles, properties), each with a
  kind you can set. A document's details list **related documents** that share them.
- **Reminders.** On a document with a due or expiry date, **Add Reminder** puts one in Reminders. Nothing
  is created until you click, and macOS asks for access the first time.
- **Multi-select editing.** Select several documents to add or remove collections and tags, set a type,
  sender, or date, mark them reviewed or favorite, get suggestions again, or move them to Trash together.
- **Export** (File → Export Archive…). Writes plain files anyone can read without StowKit: originals in a folder
  per collection, their text, and a manifest in JSON and CSV. Every copied file is re-hashed and must
  match.
- **Import from paperless-ngx** (File → Import from paperless-ngx…). Reads a `document_exporter` folder
  and brings in documents with their title, date, sender, type, tags, notes, and text.
- **Fixed:** a SwiftUI table warning AppKit logged when the list grew back after clearing a search or
  filter (it says it "will become an assert").

## Good to know

- 1.3.0 and 1.4.0 update themselves to this version (StowKit → Check for Updates…).
- Filing rules, saved views, kinds, and added reminders stay on the Mac where you made them; they don't
  sync.

## Installation

Open `StowKit-1.5.0.dmg` and drag StowKit to Applications, replacing the old version. Requires
macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple; use the SHA-256 sidecar to
verify the download.
