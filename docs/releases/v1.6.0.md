# StowKit 1.6.0

A home page to open onto, and search that can cover the whole archive from wherever you are.

## Before you install: what is not proven

- **Tested on one Mac only**, the owner's (Apple Silicon, macOS 27), on an archive of nine documents.
  Household sharing is still experimental and untested with two iCloud accounts.
- The home page's "Needs you" chips were checked against tests for every count; in the running app only
  the empty state and a single "Due in 30 days" chip were seen. Nothing here has been tried on a large
  archive, where the extra counting queries behind each search have not been measured.
- The page was verified on a build without iCloud, so its iCloud status line is untried.
- Everything 1.5.0 listed as unproven still is: Export, the paperless-ngx importer, Add Reminder,
  multi-select editing, saved views, and renaming a tag have not been used on a real archive.

## What's new

- **Overview**, first in the sidebar and where StowKit now opens (⇧⌘H, or the Home button in the
  toolbar). It has a search field over the whole archive, then **Needs you** — Inbox, overdue, due in
  30 days, expiring in 90 days, and documents in no collection — then what you added recently, your
  collections with counts, and your archive's totals. Every count opens exactly the documents behind
  it. It is called Overview because Home is one of your collections.
- **Search everywhere.** Searching inside the Inbox, Favorites, or a collection now shows a control
  above the list: **In <that place>** or **Everywhere**. **⇧⌘F** widens a search from anywhere, and a
  search with nothing in the current place tells you how many matches exist elsewhere and offers them
  in one click. Widening changes the place only — your filter chips still apply, and Trash is searched
  only from Trash.
- **Fixed:** the sidebar scrolled through the archive status in its bottom-left corner, so rows showed
  behind the text.

## Good to know

- **⌘K is now ⇧⌘F.** ⌘F still puts the cursor in the search field for the current view.
- 1.3.0 and later update themselves to this version (StowKit → Check for Updates…).
- The archive format and the sync format are unchanged from 1.5.0; a Mac on 1.5.0 keeps syncing with
  one on 1.6.0. Macs on 1.4 or earlier still stall on 1.5's document fields until they update.

## Installation

Open `StowKit-1.6.0.dmg` and drag StowKit to Applications, replacing the old version. Requires
macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple; use the SHA-256 sidecar to
verify the download.
