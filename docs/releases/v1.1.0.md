# StowKit 1.1.0

Choose which archive you're using, keep iCloud archives on your Mac only when you need them, and
delete documents for good.

## Before you install: what is not proven

- **Permanent deletion has been tested only against a simulated iCloud**, not yet between two
  real Macs. It asks for confirmation and cannot be undone — try it on something unimportant first.
- **Household sharing is still experimental** and untested with two iCloud accounts.
- **Remove Download** has been tested against a simulated iCloud, not a real one.
- Tested on Apple Silicon running macOS 27 only.

## What's new

- **Archive picker.** The button at the bottom of the sidebar shows which archive you're in and
  switches between this Mac's archive and your iCloud archives. Also in **File → Switch Archive**.
- **Switching no longer turns iCloud off.** In 1.0.0, opening another archive silently turned off
  iCloud for the one you left. If you switched archives in 1.0.0, open Settings → iCloud on your own
  archive and turn it back on if it's off.
- **Works like iCloud Drive.** In an iCloud archive, a cloud badge marks documents that aren't on
  this Mac. **Download Now** fetches one; **Remove Download** frees the space but keeps it in iCloud.
- **Delete Permanently and Empty Trash.** In Trash, remove documents for good — from this Mac, and
  for an iCloud archive, from iCloud and every Mac that uses it. A Mac that was offline or editing
  at the same moment can't bring a deleted document back.

## Good to know

- Moving to Trash is still recoverable; only Trash's Delete Permanently and Empty Trash are final.
- Nothing about how your documents are stored changes on upgrade.

## Installation

Open `StowKit-1.1.0.dmg` and drag StowKit to Applications, replacing the old version. Requires
macOS 14 or later. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple; use the SHA-256 sidecar to
verify the download.
